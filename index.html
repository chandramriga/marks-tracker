Possible normalization uses HPRCA's published Z-Score (Mean and Standard Deviation) method, with the shift that has the highest average in these submissions as the base. The "average scorer" figure is for someone who scored exactly their shift's average. Higher or lower scorers can gain more or less, depending on how spread out the shift's marks are. Standard deviation shows how spread out the marks are. A shift with a larger SD has more variation between scorers, which changes how much a high or low scorer gains or loses in normalization.
